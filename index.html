The future of digital presence. A paradigm-shifting digital environment replacing algorithmic sorting with Organic Attention Mechanics. Content survives based purely on genuine human focus.

◎ The Value Proposition

For Users

A sanctuary from digital anxiety. No infinite scroll, no spam, no performance pressure for "likes."

For Creators & Brands

Guarantees 100% active, verified attention. If content is alive, the audience is actively reading it.

For Communities

The ultimate quality filter. The Entropy Tax eliminates trolls and shallow takes at the root.

■ Core Protocols
Protocol 01
Sustained Attention Protocol (SAP)
Passive viewport tracking measures verified reading time. As long as users are silently focused on a thought, its lifespan regenerates (+1.5s/s). Rewards deep reading and active listening.
Protocol 02
The Entropy Tax (Noise-Cancellation Engine)
Interaction friction: Every keystroke drains -0.5s from surrounding content. Forces users to weigh the value of their words against the thoughts already present.
Protocol 03
Elegant Ephemerality
Visual degradation curves: Content does not abruptly cut. It diffuses gracefully from high-contrast clarity → optical blur → abstract unicode glyphs before returning to the void.
